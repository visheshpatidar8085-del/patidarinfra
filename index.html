About Patidar Infra

More Than Buildings —
We Build Trust.

At Patidar Infra, we don't just construct buildings — we build trust, relationships, and futures. Rooted in a deep commitment to quality and craftsmanship, we specialize in delivering solutions that stand the test of time.

From private villas to commercial landmarks and full-scale infrastructure, every Patidar Infra project is guided by the same principle: build it as if it were our own. Based in the heart of Indore, we combine local understanding with world-class standards.
